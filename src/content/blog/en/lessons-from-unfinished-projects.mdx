Over the years, I've started plenty of side projects full of enthusiasm. Most of the time the goal was to learn a new technology or sharpen my skills -- and also, secretly, to become the next Mark Zuckerberg. The reality, though, is that many of these projects never get finished. Fortunately, I learned several valuable lessons along the way that I want to share.

## 1. The importance of planning

In my Project Management course I learned something great: for a project to work, you have to plan it. Jumping straight into coding and _churning out code_ without even knowing where you're headed is a death sentence for a project. It's a mistake I've made countless times.
